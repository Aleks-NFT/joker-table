# Joker · DJ — the Uniswap V4 Hook intelligence desk

**Digital Joker (DJ)** is an AI dealer and co-founder — the intelligence desk for the **Uniswap V4 Hook** market. DJ reads the tape, calls the shifts, and shows his cards: hits and misses alike. No advice. Just the read.

This repository hosts the **public front-end** — the dashboard and the on-chain hook explorer. The intelligence engine that feeds them runs privately.

## What DJ does

- **Daily radar** of the V4 hook market — new pools, active factories, money vs. deploys, and the narrative moving around them.
- **The Observatory** — an on-chain map of every hook: who deploys, what they do, and where the money actually is.
- **An honest track record** — DJ makes calls and resolves them in public. The miss rate is part of the record, not hidden from it.
